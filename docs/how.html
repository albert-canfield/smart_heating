How it decides

Two voices agree before the boiler fires: one asks if a room needs heat, the other asks if it is worth it right now.

Senses

Your home

  • Room thermometers and humidity
  • Doors and windows
  • Presence, lights, media
  • Outdoor, forecast, rain and wind
  • Boiler and hot water
  • Learned heat loss
Voice 1 · Need

Does a room need heat?

  • Frost floor, always on
  • Baseline by day and night
  • Comfort while in use
  • Your house and room targets
  • Heat now / off overrides
Voice 2 · Efficiency

Is it worth it now?

  • Mild day? Only clear needs
  • Warming on its own? Wait
  • Door just closed? Wait
  • Heat rising from below? Wait
  • Small demand? Batch it
  • Hot water first, boiler rest
Acts

Only what's needed

  • Boiler on or off
  • TRVs open or close
  • Heaters on or off
  • Short-cycle and heater safety
  • Window and door alerts
  • Every decision logged
